### Changed

- **No character limit on your messages and answers in the Billion tab.** Long pasted text (a product listing, a log) now reaches Billion whole, line breaks kept, as one `[Owner via app]` turn, and shows whole in a scrollable bubble. Only a paste over 200,000 characters is refused.
- **Long messages to Telegram are split, not cut.** Text past Telegram's 4,096-character message limit goes as consecutive messages (buttons on the last), and what does not fit in a voice caption or an answered question's edited copy follows as a message of its own.
