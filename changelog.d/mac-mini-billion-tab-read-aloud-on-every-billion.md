### Added

- **Read Billion's messages aloud in the Billion tab, and dictate your reply.** Every message from Billion has a speaker button that reads it with the browser's own voices (free, nothing leaves the browser): markdown dropped, links read as "link", Q51 as "question 51", a question's choices read at the end, long messages spoken sentence by sentence so Chrome never cuts them off; tap again to stop. *Read new messages aloud* at the top of the tab (off by default, remembered) speaks each new message as it arrives while the tab is showing, one at a time; after a reload one tap on *Resume reading* lets the page speak again. A voice picker keeps your choice. The mic beside the text box dictates into the box (interim words greyed, finished phrases added as text) with the terminal mic's limits, and never sends for you; in "Answers Q3" mode the dictated text answers Q3. On a phone, reading stops with the screen locked; Telegram voice notes cover that. See docs/BILLION.md "Read aloud and dictation in the tab".
